PDF Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Important: PDF Studio is designed to let you read, create, scan, edit, organize, and manage PDF documents. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Android application.

1. Information We Collect

PDF Studio does not require you to provide personal information simply to use the core PDF features. Depending on the features and services enabled in a particular version of the application, the app may process information such as:

  • PDF files and documents that you choose to open, create, scan, edit, or share.
  • Images or documents captured using the device camera when you choose to use scanning features.
  • Basic device, diagnostic, crash, and usage information if analytics or crash-reporting services are enabled.
  • Advertising-related information if advertisements are displayed in the app, subject to the applicable advertising provider's policies and your device settings.

2. Files and Documents

PDF Studio may request access to files or media solely to provide features that you initiate, such as selecting, opening, saving, importing, exporting, or sharing documents. Documents remain under your control. We do not sell your PDF documents or use their contents for advertising.

Where processing can be performed locally on your device, PDF Studio is designed to process the relevant document locally. If a future feature sends a document to an online service for processing, the app will provide appropriate notice and the applicable third-party service terms and privacy policy will apply.

3. Camera and Scanning

If you use the document-scanning feature, PDF Studio may request camera permission. Camera access is used to capture pages or images that you choose to scan. The camera is not accessed for unrelated purposes.

4. Advertising

If the application displays advertisements, third-party advertising providers may collect information such as advertising identifiers, approximate location, device information, and ad interaction data in accordance with their own policies and applicable law. You may have controls available through Android or the advertising provider to limit personalized advertising.

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

If analytics or crash-reporting services are included, limited technical information may be collected to help us understand application performance, diagnose crashes, and improve reliability. Such information may include application version, device type, operating-system version, crash information, and other technical diagnostics.

6. Third-Party Services

PDF Studio may use trusted third-party services for functions such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, cloud functionality, authentication, payments, or other optional services. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party service you choose to use.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to provide an enabled service, comply with legal obligations, protect the security of the application, prevent abuse or fraud, or with your direction or consent.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through PDF Studio. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Data Retention

Files stored locally by PDF Studio remain on your device until you delete them or otherwise manage them through the application or your device. Information processed by third-party services is retained according to the respective provider's policies and applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

PDF Studio is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

11. Your Choices

You can control permissions through Android settings, including access to the camera and files or media. You can also delete documents stored on your device and stop using optional features that require additional information processing.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its features, or applicable legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or PDF Studio's privacy practices, please contact the application developer through the contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.
